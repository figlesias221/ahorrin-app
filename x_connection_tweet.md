## Tips for Your Tweet:
1. **Reply to everyone** who comments - this is crucial for algorithm boost
2. **Ask questions** in your replies to start conversations
3. **Follow back** people who align with your interests
4. **Quote retweet** interesting profiles to add visibility
5. **Pin this tweet** for a few days to maximize reach
6. **Post during peak hours** (8-10 AM or 6-8 PM your timezone)

## Engagement Starters:
When replying to others, use:
- "What are you building right now?"
- "What's your biggest challenge in [their area]?"
- "Just followed! Would love to hear more about [specific thing they mentioned]"
- "This resonates! Have you tried [relevant resource/tool]?"
